# Daiku brand rules

## Logo
- **Monogram:** a calligraphic "d" whose stem curls over the bowl, joined to a "k" whose arm is a leaf. The leaf's slit runs along the leaf's true centre line; in the SVGs it's a real cut-out, so it works on any background.
- **Below 32 px,** use `monogram-small-no-slit.svg`: the slit turns to mush at small sizes.
- **Colours:** sage `#4E6450` on light, white on sage or dark. Don't recolour the leaf separately, add the old orbit swoosh or star, or stretch the mark.
- **Clear space:** at least the width of the leaf on every side.

## Wordmark
- "daiku." in Fraunces italic, SOFT 100, opsz 144, weight 400, outlined in `brand/wordmark-*.svg`. The full stop is part of the name.
- Tagline: "a place where ideas take form", in Fraunces italic and sage.

## Type
- **Display:** Fraunces italic (titles, board names, big scores)
- **Interface:** Comfortaa 600/700; use 700 below 14 px
- **Data and credits:** DM Mono

## Colour roles
- **Sage:** brand and "sourced" evidence
- **Clay `#9C5530`:** assumptions and guesses only. Never decorative.
- **Lilac `#8F82CF`:** selection
- **Butter `#F4E7B6`:** sticky notes; stays light, with dark text, in dark mode too

Full values are in `design/tokens.json`.
