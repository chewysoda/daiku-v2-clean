# Research behind v2: what designers complain about

Gathered November 2026. Some sources are thin (noted in brackets), so treat these as signals, not survey data.

| Complaint or wish | Evidence | What v2 does |
|---|---|---|
| Feeds flooded with AI images; users have to keep flagging them | Pinterest coverage (404 Media, YPulse); some longtime users moving to Cosmos and Tumblr | "Human-made only" filter, built as provenance (see pipeline warning) |
| Want calm, minimal interfaces with no likes or ads | Cosmos praised for a minimal black-and-white interface (Creative Bloq, TechRadar); Are.na's spare layout and white space (Metropolis) | Gallery-like screens, content first, no social metrics |
| Work shared without credit | Designers quoted in Business of Home; Cosmos now credits creators by default | Credit captured at save, shown under every image, "Copy credits" |
| Weak search | Milanote review: search limited to recent items (Trustpilot) [only 9 reviews] | Search by idea, colour or maker; colour-swatch search |
| Big boards slow down | Miro forum: mood board of ~3,000 images slows quickly [single thread] | Local-first, performance budget for image-heavy boards |
| Find and collect in one place | Kosmik built around this (TechCrunch) | Omnibox: paste a link, drop images, or search |
| Free client sharing without seats | Milanote alternatives roundup [competitor-written] | Client share page: no sign-in, guest comments pinned to images |
